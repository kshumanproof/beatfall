Launch list privacy

When you join the launch list, you give Beatfall permission to email you when it launches. Joining the list does not create a Beatfall account.

What we collect

Your email address, when you signed up, your permission to receive the launch email, and any campaign label in the link you used, such as Story Launchbox.

We also use a coded network identifier to limit automated submissions. Your full IP address is not stored in the launch list.

How we use it

We use your email address to send the launch announcement. We use signup dates and campaign labels to understand where interest in Beatfall comes from.

Change your mind?

To leave the list or request removal of your information, email support@beatfall.app.
