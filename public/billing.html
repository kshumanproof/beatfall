How billing works

Last updated 8 October 2026. This page explains your subscription and credits. See the Terms for the full agreement.

The short version

$15 a month, or $149 a year. Start with a 14-day free trial. No payment card required.

Adding and organizing notes never uses credits. Credits are only for the writing help: conversations about an empty beat, idea sets, loglines, character interviews, and reading in a notes file.

On a plan you get 75 credits a month. They reset on the same day each month, the day you signed up. You can buy 25 more for $6, and bought credits never expire.

Your trial includes 25 credits and access to all features. That covers one notes import and ten beat conversations.

After your subscription ends, you cannot edit your boards, but you can still download your work. Pictures are deleted after 30 days. Your writing and purchased credits remain in your account, subject to the inactivity rule below.

Monthly credits are always used first. Purchased credits are only used after your monthly credits run out.

Unused purchased credits remain in your account if you cancel. They are removed if you delete your account or it is deleted for inactivity.

4

When you are running low

We show your remaining credits before you run out.

  • 15 left: a small count appears on your Account button, at the top right of the page. Nothing interrupts you.
  • 5 left: one message, once, which you can dismiss.
  • None left: you are told when you next reach for the writing help, not before. Everything that does not need credits keeps working exactly as it did.

During the trial, the reminders appear at 5 credits remaining and again at 2.

5

If you stop paying

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You can continue using Beatfall until then.

After your subscription ends:

  • You cannot edit your boards, but you can still download your work.
  • Your writing and unused purchased credits remain in your account, subject to the inactivity rule below.
  • Pictures are deleted after 30 days. Download any pictures you want to keep before then. Their notes and captions remain.
  • Subscribe again to resume editing your saved projects.

If a payment simply fails rather than you cancelling, the writing help pauses while Stripe retries, and your material does not go anywhere.

6

Inactive accounts

If you have no active subscription and do not sign in for six months, your account and everything in it will be deleted, including purchased credits.

We email the address on the account about a month beforehand. Signing in once resets the clock. If you want to keep the work, sign in and export it.

7

Refunds

Unless the law where you live says otherwise: subscription payments are not refunded for part of a period, and credit packs are not refunded. Use the free trial to decide whether Beatfall is right for you before subscribing.

If something has gone wrong, email support@beatfall.app.
