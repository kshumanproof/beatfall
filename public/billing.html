How billing works

Last updated 2 September 2026. This page is the plain version. The Terms are the contract, and they say the same things.

The short version

$12 a month, or $99 a year. Fourteen days free first, no card.

Putting notes on the board is free forever. Credits are only for the writing help: conversations about an empty beat, idea sets, and reading in a notes file.

You get 150 credits a month. They reset on the 1st. You can buy 50 more for $6, and bought credits never expire.

If you stop paying, the boards close. Nothing is deleted, and you can download all of your work at any time, including after you have stopped paying.

Two kinds of credit

This is the part worth reading twice, because the two behave differently on purpose.

You can never spend a credit you paid for while you still have a free one in hand. The software takes from the monthly allowance first, every time, and only spills into bought credits when the month's are used up.

So if you buy a pack during a heavy month and then have a quiet one, the pack is untouched. It is still there in six months, and it is still there if you cancel and come back.

4

When you are running low

We tell you before it matters, and we count in credits rather than percentages, because "eight left" means something and "95% used" does not.

  • 30 left: a small count appears next to your account, bottom left. Nothing interrupts you.
  • 10 left: one message, once, which you can dismiss.
  • None left: you are told when you next reach for the writing help, not before. Everything that does not need credits keeps working exactly as it did.

5

If you stop paying

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, not straight away. If you cancel on the 15th and you are paid up to the 30th, nothing changes until the 30th.

After that, the boards close. A subscription is what gives you access to them. But:

  • Nothing is deleted. Every project, card, note and outline stays exactly where it is.
  • You can still export everything, at any time, including after your plan has ended. One file, no questions, no conditions.
  • Bought credits stay in your account. They do not burn and we do not take them back. If you subscribe again they are waiting.
  • Subscribe again and everything opens exactly as you left it.

If a payment simply fails rather than you cancelling, the writing help pauses while Stripe retries, and your material does not go anywhere.

6

The six month rule

An account that nobody signs into for six months, and that has no live subscription, is deleted along with everything in it. Every board, every note, every outline, and any credits left in it.

We email the address on the account about a month beforehand. Signing in once resets the clock. If you want to keep the work, sign in and export it.

This is deliberate and it runs on a schedule rather than being something we get around to. We would rather not be sitting on a stranger's unfinished screenplay indefinitely, and if we say we delete dormant accounts then it has to be a thing that actually happens.

7

Refunds

Unless the law where you live says otherwise: subscription payments are not refunded for part of a period, and credit packs are not refunded. The 14 day trial exists precisely so you can find out whether this works for you before any money changes hands.

If something has gone genuinely wrong, write to kris@beatfall.app and a person will read it.
