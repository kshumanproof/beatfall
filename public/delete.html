Delete your Beatfall account

This removes your account and everything in it. It cannot be undone.

  • Every script and every board.
  • Every note, including anything still waiting from your phone.
  • Your sign in. The same email address would start over from nothing.
  • Your subscription, which is cancelled as part of this.

If you only want your work out of Beatfall, sign in first and download it from Settings. Once this is done there is nothing left to download.

We send a code rather than deleting on the strength of a typed address. An account is not something a stranger should be able to remove by knowing your email.

Check your email. A six digit code is on its way to . It works once and lasts an hour.

Changed your mind? Go back to your boards.

Your account has been deleted. Everything in it is gone, and there is nothing left here that belongs to you. Thank you for trying Beatfall.

If you signed in on a phone, sign out there or delete the app; it is holding nothing but a session that no longer works.

If you cannot get to that email address any more, write to support@beatfall.app from whichever address you can, tell us which account you mean, and we will handle it by hand.
