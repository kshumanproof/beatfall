Getting notes into Beatfall

Start from a notes file

Open the project menu beside its title and choose Paste in your notes. Paste text or drop in a .txt or .md file. Beatfall shows you the results before adding anything to the board.

Add one note

Type the note in the bar below the header, then choose Place it. Beatfall suggests where it belongs. You make the final choice.

Working on the board

Review an uncertain placement

Choose the suggested beat that fits. If none fits, choose Set it aside for now. The note stays with the project until you decide.

Move or settle a card

Drag a card to another beat, or use its move control. Choose the circle on a card to mark that placement as settled so Beatfall will not move it later.

Work on an empty beat

Choose What goes here? under the empty beat. Beatfall asks about that specific gap and builds a card from your answers only after you approve it.

Change the story structure

Open the project menu and choose a structure from the list. Beatfall places every board card into the new structure and shows you what moved. Notes you set aside stay set aside.

Your account and work

Download everything

Open Account → Settings → Your data, then choose Download. The file contains every project, card, and note in your account.

Understand credits

Adding, moving, and organizing notes is free. Conversations and idea sets use one credit. Reading a notes file uses two. See How billing works for the full rules.

Recover from a mistake

Choose Undo in the confirmation bar, or press Ctrl+Z on Windows and Command+Z on Mac. Undo is available until you refresh or close the page.
