How it works

From loose notes to a story you can see.

Add your notes

Type one thought at a time or paste in the whole file. Nothing needs to be organized first.

Beatfall sorts them

Story beats go to the board. Character, dialogue, research, and other material stays in Notes.

Work on the gaps

Empty beats stay visible. When you want help, Beatfall asks questions instead of inventing your story.

Your work stays yours

Help seeing your story, not software writing it.

Beatfall is built for writers who want structure without handing over authorship.

Your words remain your words.Beatfall organizes your material. It does not rewrite your screenplay.
Uncertainty stays visible.If a placement is not clear, Beatfall asks or leaves the note aside.
Your writing isn’t used to train AI.Your projects are private to your account and can be exported or deleted.
