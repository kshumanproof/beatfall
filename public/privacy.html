Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026. Beatfall, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company.

The short version

Yes, your projects are saved. That's the product: your boards follow you between machines. Anyone who tells you a tool like this stores nothing is describing a different tool.

They're saved privately. Every row in our database is locked to one account, by the database itself. No other user can reach your work. Our own admin tools can count your cards but cannot read them.

Nothing you write trains a model. Not ours, not Anthropic's, not anyone's.

No ads, no trackers, no data sales. Beatfall makes money one way: people pay $12 a month for it.

You can take it all with you, any time. One button in Settings, no conditions.

1

What we hold

  • Your email address. It's how you sign in: we email you a link. There is no password, so we hold no password.
  • A display name, if you give us one.
  • Your material. Project titles, structures, loglines and briefs, every card and note, and everything you write in the outline. Stored as structured data on one row per project.
  • Usage counts. One record per AI call: which kind of call it was, how many credits and tokens it used, what it cost us, and when. These records contain none of your writing: no card text, no prompts, no responses.
  • A few product events, so we can tell whether the thing works: which option you picked on the welcome screen, and your billing state changes. Nothing else, and no story text.
  • Billing identifiers from Stripe: a customer ID, a subscription ID and its status. We never see or store your card number.
  • Ordinary server logs kept by our hosting provider, which include IP addresses, as any web server does.

We don't ask for your real name, address, phone number or date of birth, and we'd rather you didn't give them to us.

2

Who can actually see your writing

You can. Every projects, usage and profile row is protected by row-level security in Postgres: a signed-in account can read its own rows and is refused every other row. That's enforced beneath our application code, not by it, so a bug in the app can't quietly hand your work to another user.

Other users cannot. There is no sharing feature, no public board, no team workspace, no discovery, no feed. Your projects exist for exactly one account.

We can't, in the ordinary course. The administrative dashboard we use to run the business is built so it cannot request the text of your cards. It reports counts, costs and dates. This is a property of the code, not a policy we're asking you to trust.

The three narrow exceptions (you ask us for help, the law compels us, or we're investigating a specific security incident or abuse report) are set out in section 4 of the Terms. We don't review accounts routinely, at random, or out of curiosity.

3

What leaves the app, and when

Placing a note on the board with the built-in matcher happens entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

When you use a feature that needs the AI (reading in a notes file, a conversation about a beat, an idea set, help with a logline, or a closer look at where a note belongs) we send Anthropic the text that feature needs: the note or file you're working on, the beats around it, and your project's logline and brief where they'd change the answer. We do not send your email address or any account identifier with it.

Anthropic, under its commercial terms: does not use this to train its models, and deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days. Content its automated systems flag as a usage-policy violation is kept longer, up to two years, which is why the acceptable-use rules in the Terms exist.

5

Cookies and what's kept in your browser

Beatfall sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage holds your sign-in session so you don't have to click a fresh email link on every visit, your light/dark preference, and your default structure. Clearing your browser data signs you out; it doesn't touch your projects.

6

How long we keep things

  • Your material: until you delete it, or delete your account, or the account goes six months without a sign-in and without a live subscription. At that point the account and everything in it is deleted automatically. We email you about a month beforehand, and signing in resets the clock. This is a job that runs, not an intention: we would rather not be holding a stranger's unfinished screenplay indefinitely.
  • Usage and billing records: kept while your account exists and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting rules require. They contain no story text.
  • Server logs: on our host's ordinary retention schedule.
  • Backups: routine encrypted backups are overwritten on our providers' rolling cycle, so a deleted project can persist in a backup briefly after you delete it.

7

What you can do about all of this

Two of these are buttons rather than requests, which we think is the right way round:

  • Take it with you. Settings → Your data → Download everything. Every project, card, note and outline in one file. No conditions and no waiting on us.
  • Delete it. Delete a single project from the dashboard, or your whole account from Settings → Profile. Account deletion removes your profile, projects, usage and events from the live database immediately and cannot be undone. Export first.
  • Correct it, or ask what we hold. Email privacy@beatfall.app. The export button will generally answer the question faster than we can.

Depending on where you live (the EEA and UK, California, and a growing number of US states) you may have statutory rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection, and a right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We honour these requests from everyone, wherever you are, rather than checking your postcode first. For EEA and UK users, our lawful bases are performance of our contract with you (running the Service), our legitimate interests (keeping it secure and working out what it costs), and legal obligation (tax records). You may complain to your local supervisory authority.

8

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit and data is encrypted at rest. Access is by emailed sign-in link, so there is no password of yours for us to lose. Access to production systems is limited to the one person who runs Beatfall and protected by multi-factor authentication.

No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your material, we'll tell you what happened, what was affected and what to do about it, without waiting to be forced to.

9

Children

Beatfall is for adults; you must be 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, email privacy@beatfall.app and we'll remove it.

10

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects how your material is handled, we'll email you before it takes effect, not after. The effective date at the top always tells you which version you're reading.
